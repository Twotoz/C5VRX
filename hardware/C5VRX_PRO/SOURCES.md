# Sources, revisions and licenses

This proposal is a contribution to **C5VRX by Twotoz and the C5VRX contributors**,
[canonical repository](https://github.com/Twotoz/C5VRX),
[official website and Discord invite](https://twotoz.github.io/C5VRX/).
Base: `e0f49b5` (2026-10-28 checkout). It preserves the DIY V3 outline and
bay pad coordinates and adapts its passive video DAC to precision SMD parts.
It does not assert that a fabricated receiver has been validated.

| Source | Use / changes |

Primary links are in [COMPONENTS.md](COMPONENTS.md). The Qorvo Rev F PDF used
for the circuit was the manufacturer's document mirrored by its distributor:
[QPL9504TR7.pdf](https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/6296/QPL9504TR7.pdf).

Standard footprints and existing standard STEP references derive from the
KiCad 10 libraries, under the KiCad library CC-BY-SA 4.0 license with its
design exception: [license](https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/).
Copies in `PRO.pretty` change model references, remove crowded silkscreen
graphics to the fabrication layer and retain the physical lands. Custom
package envelopes are generated by `tools/build_models.py`, not downloaded
manufacturer solids; SMA threads are illustrative rings.

`PRO.pretty/SW_SKRHABE010_SMD.kicad_mod` derives from
[Antmicro hardware-components](https://github.com/antmicro/hardware-components/blob/main/kicad-footprints/sw-skrhabe010-smd/SW_SKRHABE010_SMD.kicad_mod).
The pad geometry is preserved; cosmetic silk is moved to the fabrication
layer and the model replaced with a simple parametric envelope. Antmicro's
Apache-2.0 license is retained in [licenses/Antmicro-Apache-2.0.txt](licenses/Antmicro-Apache-2.0.txt).

An Espressif KiCad library checkout was consulted to cross-check the SoC
package recommendation; the new schematic symbols are generated physical
pin tables, not copied Espressif symbol artwork. These symbols deliberately
use passive pin types; ERC does not check driver or power direction.

The unrelated SILOAD render scripts in the user's workspace were inspected
for installed rendering tools only. Their design, branding and code are not
incorporated in this board. No AI-generated image is used as evidence of PCB
geometry or as a manufacturing asset.
